# OMX30 Strategy Notes

## Overview

This document covers the adaptation of the two ATOS US strategies to the
**OMX Stockholm 30** universe. The OMX30 module is intentionally isolated
from the US module — separate files, separate backtest, separate docs.

## Why Each Strategy Fails on OMX30

### OMX Blend — Sharpe -0.07, CAGR -0.8%

The momentum Blend is structurally mis-matched to OMX30:

1. **Too few stocks, too correlated.** The 30-stock universe has high
   cross-correlation (same economy, same macro, same currency). In the US,
   61 diversified stocks give the momentum ranking a real spread to exploit.
   On OMX30, most stocks move together — the "top 6 vs bottom" distinction
   shrinks dramatically.

2. **Weekly rebalance friction kills returns.** 377 sell events over 3 years
   = ~10 rebalance trades per month. At 0.08% round-trip on 8 positions,
   the transaction cost alone wipes the slim alpha. The US version works
   because momentum alpha on 61 diverse stocks is thick enough to survive it.

3. **OMX30 had a strong buy-and-hold period (CAGR 12.5%).** A strategy that
   keeps switching out of winners for "better" momentum names keeps booking
   gains too early and re-entering at higher prices.

**Conclusion:** The Blend's design requires a large, diversified universe.
OMX30 (30 highly-correlated Swedish stocks) is too small. **Do not run live.**

---

### OMX Reversion — Sharpe 0.66, CAGR +3.7%

The Reversion strategy shows *genuine edge* on OMX30 but not enough to clear
the bar yet:

**Positives:**
- Win rate 65.2% — same as the US version (66%), signal quality transfers
- Positive P&L (+18,510 SEK on 150K sleeve = +12.3% total)
- Max drawdown -6.7% — very controlled
- All 4 signal conditions (EMA200, RSI<33, dip>5%, vol spike) are filtering
  correctly — these are not random wins

**Why it fails the bar:**
- Sharpe 0.66 < 0.80 threshold — not enough return per unit of risk yet
- Only 23 closed trades in 3 years ≈ 7-8 per year
- OMX30 has fewer capitulation volume spikes than US stocks
  (lower retail participation, more institutional holders)
- CAGR +3.7% vs buy-and-hold +12.5% — opportunity cost is significant

**The core problem:** The VOL_MULT = 1.5x filter is correct in principle
but fires too rarely on Swedish blue-chips. Institutions unwind positions
gradually; the panic-selling volume signature that works on US retail-heavy
stocks is rarer on OMX30.

## Recommendations

### For OMX Blend

Option A — **Don't run it.** Use the US Blend allocation on US stocks where
it has a validated Sharpe of 1.30. The OMX30 Blend has no edge here.

Option B — **Monthly rebalance, larger threshold.** Change `REBAL_DAYS = 30`
and `MOM_THRESHOLD = 0.10` (10% 6-month return) to slash friction and demand
stronger signals. Needs re-backtest before enabling.

Option C — **Sector overlay.** Instead of pure stocks, use OMX30 as a
filter for the sector rotation within a Nordic equity sleeve — requires
adding sector data to the universe.

### For OMX Reversion

The edge is real (65% win rate) but needs to be made *more selective* or the
universe extended:

1. **Relax the volume filter slightly** — try `VOL_MULT = 1.2` instead of
   1.5 to increase signal frequency on less-liquid Swedish stocks. Needs grid
   search to confirm it doesn't let garbage through.

2. **Widen the dip threshold** — try `DIP_PCT = 0.06` (6% below SMA20)
   to ensure only deep, real capitulation dips qualify.

3. **Extend the universe** — add the CPH25 (Danish) or Nordic Mid-Cap
   stocks. A 60-80 stock Nordic universe would give the reversion scanner
   more opportunities and a more meaningful win-rate sample.

4. **Wait for more data.** 23 trades in 3 years is statistically thin.
   Run the backtest again in 6-12 months with a 4-5 year window.

## How to Re-run the Backtest

```bash
# Both strategies (default 3 years)
python omx30_backtest.py

# Reversion only, 5-year window
python omx30_backtest.py --strategy rev --years 5

# Blend only
python omx30_backtest.py --strategy blend

# Force fresh data download (ignore cache)
python omx30_backtest.py --no-cache
```

Output files written to `data/`:
- `omx30_backtest_trades.csv` — every trade: date, strategy, ticker, side, P&L, reason
- `omx30_backtest_equity.csv` — daily equity: blend, reversion, combined, benchmark

## Decision

**2026-08-08:** Neither OMX30 strategy meets the pass criteria.
- OMX Blend: structurally unsuited to a 30-stock correlated universe
- OMX Reversion: promising signal quality but insufficient return and frequency

**Action:** Keep US strategies live. Monitor OMX Reversion every 6 months
as more data accumulates. Do not allocate capital to OMX30 until Sharpe ≥ 0.8
on a ≥ 4-year backtest window.
